GRAND TABLE CASINO

Hold'em · Craps · Roulette

How to start

  1. Choose Solo (you vs. house & AI players) or Pass & Play (two of you on one phone).
  2. Each player starts with $1,000. Banks save automatically on this device.
  3. Pick a table below. Tap a chip, then tap the felt to bet.
  4. In Hold'em Pass & Play, hand the phone over when asked — cards stay hidden until the next player taps Reveal.
  5. Tap ‹ Lobby any time. Broke? Reset banks at the bottom.
